Merchant lots are for a seller with stock in several Steam accounts and software of his own that sends trade offers. You send us **lots**; we put them on the CSBoard P2P market; when one is bought we call your server; you send the Steam offer and tell us what happened to it.

You do not connect any Steam account to CSBoard and we do not ask for a login, an API key or a trade link of your accounts.

<Note>
  Off by default. Write to us to have it switched on for your merchant account, and give us the three URLs described below.
</Note>

## The numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Time to send the offer | **12 hours** from the purchase, every lot |
| Buyer's time to accept | 24 hours from the moment the offer is out |
| Buyer may cancel | after 30 minutes with no offer |
| Missed send | the order is cancelled and the buyer refunded. **No fine.** The miss is counted in your delivery record |
| Commission | **1%** of the sale price |
| Payout | to your CSBoard balance **14 days** after the buyer accepted, if nothing went wrong |
| Faster payout | send the seller account's Steam refresh token with your status reports: once we read the trade as complete after Steam's 7-day protection, the sale is paid without waiting for day 14 |
| Reversal by your account | the buyer is refunded in full, your merchant account is closed for good and its balance is frozen |

Only items that can be traded right now are accepted (`assetType: 1`).

## Three URLs on your side

| URL | We call it when | You answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Order Create** | a lot of yours is being bought, before the buyer's money moves | `{"status": 1, "orderId": "<your id>", "errorMsg": ""}` to take the order, `status: 2` to refuse |
| **Order Details** | we want the order's current status | `{"requestId", "orderStatus", "reversedType", "tradeOfferStatus", "tradeOfferId"}`, `orderStatus: -1` if you do not know the order |
| **Order Cancellation** | the buyer gave up waiting | `{"cancelSuccess": true, "message": ""}`; answer `false` if an offer is already out |

All three are `POST` with a JSON body. You have **5 seconds** to answer Order Create (3 seconds when the buyer comes from a partner market). No answer in time means the purchase does not happen, so the same item listed on several markets cannot be sold twice: every sale asks you first.

### Verifying our calls

Every body carries `sign`: take every other field, sort by name, join as `name=value` with `&`, append `&sign=<your app secret>`, MD5, upper case. A `null` value is the string `null`.

```text
payPrice=12.34&productId=sku-1&requestId=ms3f9c...&tradeUrl=https://steamcommunity.com/tradeoffer/new/?partner=1&token=a&sign=<app secret>
```

The same body is also signed in the `X-CSBoard-Signature` header (`t=<unix>,v1=<hex>`, HMAC-SHA256 of `<t>.<raw body>` with the same secret), exactly like our [webhooks](/guides/webhooks).

## How a sale goes

<Steps>
  <Step title="List the lots">
    ```bash
    curl https://csboard.com/v1/p2p/sell/create \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer csb_pub_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "appId": 730,
        "assetType": 1,
        "products": [{
          "productId": "sku-1",
          "steamId": "76561198000000001",
          "assetId": "30000000001",
          "classId": "310777179",
          "instanceId": "0",
          "marketHashName": "AK-47 | Redline (Field-Tested)",
          "price": 12.34,
          "tradableTime": 0,
          "inspectUrl": "steam://rungame/730/..."
        }]
      }'
    ```

    `productId` is your own id for the lot. Up to 100 products per call. A product we cannot take comes back in `errorData.failList` with its reason; the others are accepted.

    A lot is not on sale yet. We first read the Steam account's **public** inventory ourselves and list the lot once we have seen the item there. Follow it with `GET /p2p/sell/detail/{productId}`: `operationStatus` goes `100` (listing) to `101` (listed) or `102` (failed, with `failedCode`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the order">
    We call your Order Create URL with `requestId` (our id for the sale, use it in every report), your `productId`, `payPrice` and the buyer's `tradeUrl`. Answer `status: 1` and send the Steam offer to that trade link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report every status">
    ```bash
    curl https://csboard.com/v1/p2p/order/status/notify \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer csb_pub_..." -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{"requestId": "ms3f9c...", "orderStatus": 3, "tradeOfferId": "8123456789", "tradeOfferStatus": 2}'
    ```

    Send one each time the status changes. Repeating a report is safe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    `GET /p2p/sell/order/list` shows every sale with our `orderStatus`, our `fee` and `settleTime`, the moment it pays to your balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order statuses

| `orderStatus` | Meaning | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | waiting for delivery | |
| `2` | offer created, waiting for your Steam confirmation | `tradeOfferId` |
| `3` | offer is out, waiting for the buyer | `tradeOfferId` |
| `10` | the buyer accepted | |
| `11` | cancelled | with an offer out: `tradeOfferStatus`, the Steam state that ended it (`5` expired, `6` cancelled, `7` declined, `4` countered, `10` cancelled by second factor) |
| `200` | Steam's protection is over, the trade was not reversed | |
| `220` | reversed | `reversedType`: `1` the seller, `2` the buyer |

`tradeOfferStatus` is Steam's own `ETradeOfferState`.

What we do with a report:

- `10` is checked against the seller's public inventory. If the item is still there, the report is refused with `asset_still_in_inventory`.
- `11` after an offer never closes the sale on your word alone: we look for the item back in the seller's inventory first.
- `220` with `reversedType: 2` puts the sale on hold for a manual review. It does not pay.

## The Steam refresh token

Add `refreshToken` to a status report to let us read that one trade on Steam:

```json
{"requestId": "ms3f9c...", "orderStatus": 200, "refreshToken": "eyJ..."}
```

- It must be the **mobile** refresh token of the Steam account that sent the offer.
- We use it for one read and discard it. It is not stored and not logged, and we never ask Steam to renew it, so your copy stays valid.
- With `10` we check the offer carries the sold item and was accepted by this sale's buyer. With `200`, sent after Steam's protection has ended, we read the trade: complete means the sale is paid now; reversed means it is settled as a reversal.
- The answer tells you what the read proved in `tokenRead`: `accepted`, `complete`, `reversed`, `not_final` (too early, send `200` again later), `mismatch` (another account's token, or an offer or trade that does not give this sale's item to this sale's buyer), `token_refused`, `unreadable`.

The token is optional. Without it the sale pays on day 14.

Keep the seller account's inventory public until the sale is paid. On day 14 we look at it once more; if we cannot read it, the payout waits and we look again every hour.

## Managing lots

| Call | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /p2p/sell/update` | new prices; `requestId` makes a retry safe; answers `successList` and `failList` |
| `POST /p2p/sell/cancel` | take lots off sale; a lot being bought right now answers `lot_in_trade` |
| `GET /p2p/sell/detail/{productId}` | one lot |
| `GET /p2p/sell/list` | your lots, plus `deliveryInfo`: delivered share, average send time and missed sends over 7 and 15 days |
| `POST /p2p/sell/price/reference` | our lowest live ask per `marketHashName` |

Lot `status`: `1` selling, `2` cancelled, `3` hidden (we stopped seeing the item in the account; it returns by itself), `4` in a trade, `5` sold.

A price under half of our market price is refused with `price_below_market`, as for any P2P listing.

## Answers

Every answer has the same shape:

```json
{"success": true, "data": {}, "errorCode": 0, "errorMsg": null, "errorData": null, "errorCodeStr": null}
```

On a refusal `success` is `false`, `errorCodeStr` is the code to branch on and `errorMsg` says it in words.

| `errorCodeStr` | When |
|---|---|
| `selling_not_enabled` | merchant lots are not switched on for this account |
| `merchant_terminated` | the account was closed after a reversal |
| `cooldown_not_supported` | the item is not tradable yet |
| `steam_account_in_use` | another account already lists from this Steam account |
| `asset_already_listed` | the item is already in an open lot |
| `asset_not_in_inventory` | we did not find the item in the account's public inventory |
| `trade_offer_id_required` | `2`, `3` or `10` without an offer on record |
| `trade_offer_status_required` | `11` with an offer out and no Steam state |
| `order_not_delivered` | `200` or `220` before `10` |
| `rate_limit_exceeded` | 30 lot writes or 600 status reports per minute |
